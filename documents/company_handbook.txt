Company Handbook

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role allows it and their manager has approved the arrangement. Team members should remain available during agreed core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 euros for approved courses, books, conferences, or certification exams related to their role. Requests should be discussed with the manager before purchase.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and standard accessories. Additional ergonomic equipment may be requested when there is a demonstrated work need.
