Product Guide

Starter plan
The Starter plan supports up to 5 team members and includes basic reporting, email support, and 10 GB of shared storage.

Professional plan
The Professional plan supports up to 25 team members. It includes advanced reporting, priority email support, API access, and 100 GB of shared storage.

Enterprise plan
The Enterprise plan is intended for larger organizations. It includes custom user limits, single sign-on, dedicated support, and negotiated storage limits. Pricing is provided by the sales team.
